Research Experience

  • 2026 - 2026

    Xi'an, China

    Research Assistant
    Xi'an Jiaotong University
    July–August 2026. Motorized Catheter Management Module for Industrial Inspection; advisor, Prof. Qiji Ze.
    • Designed a motorized translating-pulley catheter accumulator for insertion and retraction, with approximately twofold length compensation (ΔL ≈ 2Δx).
    • Developed the assembly in Fusion 360 and SolidWorks using a 120-mm guide pulley, 608 bearings, an MGN12H carriage, a T8 lead-screw stage, and custom 3D-printed components.
    • Implemented Python/Tkinter software, USB serial communication, Arduino UNO firmware, and TB6600 stepper control, including calibration, jogging, fixed-distance motion, adjustable speed and acceleration, travel limits, and watchdog protection.
  • 2026 - 2026

    Hong Kong SAR

    Research Assistant
    The Chinese University of Hong Kong
    June–July 2026. Magnetically Steerable Micro-Camera Catheter for Bronchial-Tree Phantom Navigation; advisor, Prof. Hongliang Ren.
    • Designed and fabricated a millimeter-scale catheter integrating an LED micro-camera, a 0.2-mm nitinol support, heat-shrink encapsulation, and a hard-magnetic Ecoflex cuff.
    • Built insertion-force and steering-characterization setups and completed bronchial-tree phantom navigation experiments.
    • Demonstrated multidirectional magnetic steering and forward-looking intraluminal imaging; led manuscript preparation as co-first author.
  • 2026 - 2026

    Zurich, Switzerland

    Research Assistant (Semester Project)
    ETH Zurich, Multi-Scale Robotics Lab
    March–June 2026. Soft Miniaturized Intraductal Imaging Catheter; supervisor, Prof. Bradley Nelson.
    • Designed and fabricated flexible imaging catheter prototypes using nitinol support wires of 0.5 mm and 0.7 mm in diameter.
    • Built a four-load-cell force platform with Arduino and Python data acquisition and compared prototypes with a 1.1-mm rigid reference.
    • The reported analysis used 10 selected trials from 20 recorded manual trials per condition and included a 1.1-mm rigid reference; compared support and surface configurations in a simplified silicone setup.
    • Demonstrated imaging in a silicone breast-duct phantom; semester project grade 5.5/6.0.
