The Kiri-Capsule is a 17-mm biopsy capsule that uses a dual-cam mechanism to deploy polyimide kirigami flaps and perform controlled rotational scraping. The design aims to extend wireless capsule endoscopy from passive imaging to histology-ready tissue acquisition.

**My role:** **Second author; Ruizhou Zhao and Yichen Chu are co-first authors.** My contribution centered on motor-control electronics, Arduino pulse-control logic, angular calibration, iterative system integration, ex vivo experiments, quantitative analysis, and validation writing.

## Key results

- Built an angular calibration platform with approximately **18.1 degrees per pulse** and **R-squared = 0.99**.
- Measured shallow and repeatable tissue penetration with a median depth of approximately **0.61 mm**.
- Ex vivo porcine experiments yielded mean biopsy masses of approximately **10.9 mg** for stomach and **18.9 mg** for small intestine, with preserved mucosal and submucosal tissue.
- Demonstrated tissue-interaction forces within reported safe ranges for gastrointestinal biopsy.
