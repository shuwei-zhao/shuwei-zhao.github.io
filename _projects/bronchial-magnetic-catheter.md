**June–July 2026 · Research Assistant · The Chinese University of Hong Kong · Advisor: Prof. Hongliang Ren**

This project develops a millimeter-scale catheter that combines distal visualization with controllable magnetic steering for navigation in branched lumens. The system integrates an LED micro-camera, a 0.2-mm nitinol support wire, heat-shrink encapsulation, and a wrapped hard-magnetic Ecoflex cuff actuated by an external permanent magnet.

**My role:** I designed and fabricated the catheter, integrated the imaging and magnetic components, developed the insertion-force and steering-characterization experiments, conducted bronchial-tree phantom navigation, and led manuscript preparation as a **co-first author**.

## Key results

- Characterized insertion force under dry and saline-wetted conditions and evaluated multidirectional magnetic steering and forward-looking imaging in a bronchial-tree phantom.
- Achieved multidirectional distal steering of **30.2 degrees left**, **35.0 degrees right**, **25.6 degrees up**, and **35.5 degrees down**.
- Demonstrated magnetically assisted redirection at primary and secondary bronchial bifurcations while maintaining forward-looking intraluminal imaging.
