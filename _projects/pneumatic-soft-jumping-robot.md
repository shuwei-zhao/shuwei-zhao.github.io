**Ongoing since September 2025 · Research Assistant · The Chinese University of Hong Kong · Advisor: Prof. Hongliang Ren**

This project studies pneumatic actuation and elastic-energy storage in a **double-layer spherical-cap silicone structure**. The prototype achieves repeatable jumping in benchtop experiments.

## My contribution

- Designed and fabricated the silicone actuator and developed geometric models for the layer-junction coordinates, actuator radius, and opening angle.
- Implemented Arduino air-pump control and pressure-release logic for inflation and release cycles.
- Evaluated material stiffness and actuation cycles against jump height, frequency, and repeatability, and measured and analyzed pressure variations during jumping.

The work remains in progress. The current research examines how actuator geometry, material stiffness, and pneumatic control affect the jumping motion.
