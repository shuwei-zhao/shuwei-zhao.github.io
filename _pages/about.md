I am **Shuwei Zhao (Wayne)**, a final-year undergraduate student in Electronic Engineering at [The Chinese University of Hong Kong](https://www.cuhk.edu.hk/), graduating in May 2027. My research focuses on **medical robotics, surgical robotics, and magnetic robotics**, with an emphasis on miniaturized systems for minimally invasive imaging and intervention.

At CUHK, I work with **Prof. Hongliang Ren** in [Ren Lab](http://www.labren.org/mm/) on magnetic imaging catheters, capsule robots, and soft robotic systems. In 2026, I completed an exchange semester at [ETH Zürich](https://ethz.ch/en.html) and a research project in its Multi-Scale Robotics Lab under **Prof. Bradley Nelson**, developing and evaluating flexible intraductal imaging catheter prototypes. I also worked as a **Research Assistant at Xi'an Jiaotong University during July–August 2026**, where I designed a motorized catheter management module and its control software for industrial inspection in **Prof. Qiji Ze's group**.

My long-term goal is to develop compact robotic systems that combine magnetic actuation, compliant mechanics, sensing, and rigorous experimental validation to make minimally invasive diagnosis and intervention safer and more capable. I am applying to PhD programs beginning in **Fall 2027**.

- Benchtop, phantom, and ex vivo validation of medical robotic devices
