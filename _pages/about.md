I am **Shuwei Zhao (Wayne)**, a final-year undergraduate student in Electronic Engineering at [**The Chinese University of Hong Kong**](https://www.cuhk.edu.hk/), graduating in May 2027. My research experience spans robotic hardware design, actuation, embedded control, and experimental validation. Building on projects in magnetic and soft robotics, I am particularly interested in pursuing PhD research on **cable-driven mechanisms, compliant robots, and wearable robotic systems**.

My research experience spans CUHK, Xi’an Jiaotong University, and ETH Zürich. At CUHK, I work with [**Prof. Hongliang Ren**](http://www.labren.org/mm/) on magnetic imaging catheters, capsule robots, and soft robotic systems. During July–August 2026, I worked as a **Research Assistant** in [**Prof. Qiji Ze’s group at Xi’an Jiaotong University**](https://gr.xjtu.edu.cn/zeqiji/), where I designed and built a motorized catheter-management module and developed its embedded and PC-based motion-control software for an industrial-inspection platform. At [**ETH Zürich’s Multi-Scale Robotics Lab**](https://msrl.ethz.ch/), I completed a semester project under **Prof. Bradley Nelson**, developing flexible catheter prototypes and a calibrated force-measurement platform for experimental evaluation.

My long-term goal is to develop robotic systems that combine effective mechanical design, actuation, sensing, and intelligent control to assist people in physical tasks. I am especially interested in how cable-driven and compliant mechanisms can enable lightweight, adaptable wearable robots. I aim to build on my strengths in hardware development and system integration while developing deeper expertise in modeling, control, and learning-based methods. I am applying to PhD programs beginning in **Fall 2027**.
